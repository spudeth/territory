Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

Territory ("we") is a multiplayer browser game at territory.sarl. This page explains what we collect when you play and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Game data: your player name, the blocks you own, your squares balance, your color and skin choices, and when you claimed things. Other players can see your name and your blocks.
  • Account details (only if you create an account): your email address and a password, which is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google.
  • Technical data: your IP address and browser type. We use your IP address to limit how many new guest players one network can create, and our login provider keeps it with your login session for security.
  • Stored in your browser: a random code that remembers your guest player, and a login cookie if you log in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

Only to run the game: saving your progress, showing names and leaderboards, logging you in, sending account emails (verification codes and password resets), and stopping abuse. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who handles it for us

  • Neon: stores the game database and handles logins.
  • Render: runs the game server.
  • Cloudflare: provides our domain name service.
  • Resend: sends account emails.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google".

How long we keep it
